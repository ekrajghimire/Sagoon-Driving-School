# Customer guide — booking your driving lessons

*(Feel free to copy parts of this into an email or the website's FAQ.)*

## Book a lesson (about 2 minutes, no account needed)

1. Go to **Book a lesson** (big yellow button on every page).
2. **Choose your lesson:** 1, 1.5 or 2-hour lesson, the Driving Test package, an overseas licence refresher or a confidence lesson. All lessons are in our automatic dual-control car.
3. **Pick a date.** Days with a green dot have free times. Then tap a start time.
   - Day fully booked? Join the **waitlist** for that day and we'll email you if a spot opens.
4. **Enter your details:** name, mobile, email and where we should pick you up.
   - Have a promo, referral or gift voucher code? Enter it and tap **Apply**.
5. **Review and confirm.** You'll see a confirmation page and get an email with all the details and a calendar invite.

**Payment:** nothing to pay online. Pay at your lesson by card, cash or PayID, or by bank transfer beforehand. All prices include GST.

## Change or cancel

- Open the link in your confirmation email (or **My account → Lessons → Manage**).
- **Reschedule:** pick a new date and time. **Cancel:** tap Cancel lesson.
- Free changes up to **24 hours** before your lesson. Inside 24 hours, please call or text **0451 634 722**.

## Reminders

You'll get an email reminder **the day before** and **2 hours before** each lesson.

## Your account (optional but handy)

Create an account at **/register** (or "Continue with Google") to:

- see upcoming and past lessons in one place
- buy a **lesson package** and book each lesson with your credits
- download **tax invoices**
- save pickup addresses
- add your learner licence number and test date
- follow your **skills progress** (your instructor rates parking, roundabouts and other skills after each lesson) and read lesson notes

Booked as a guest first? Ask us and we'll link those lessons to your account.

## Packages

Pricing → choose a package → **Get this package**. Credits appear in your account straight away. Pay at your first lesson or by bank transfer using the reference shown. When booking, choose **"Use a package lesson"**.

## Before your lesson

- Bring your **learner licence** (or overseas licence). We can't start without it.
- Wear flat, comfortable shoes; bring glasses if you need them.
- Nervous? That's normal. Tell us when booking and we'll start somewhere quiet.

## Privacy

You can update your details or **delete your account** any time under **My account → Profile**.
